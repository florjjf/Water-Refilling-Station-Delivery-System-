# Diagram 2. Use Case Diagram (simplified)

## Water Refilling Station Delivery System

**Actors:**
- Owner
- Staff
- Delivery Rider

**Use Cases:**

- **Owner**
  - Manage users and station records
  - Manage customers and orders

- **Staff**
  - Record payments and view balances
  - Assign riders and manage routes

- **Delivery Rider**
  - View assigned deliveries
  - Update delivery status
